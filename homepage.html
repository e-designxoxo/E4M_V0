Ages 3–6

AKKA Montessori School

Akanin'ny Kilonga — "House of Children". Full-time Montessori early childhood education grounded in Malagasy culture.

KANTO — Culture Programme

Every Saturday: French, Malagasy, English, chess, music, piano, violin and voice for primary-age children.
